# v0.5.0 — Diamond and Master baselines

The bundled ranked dataset is independently reconciled against the full selected
source and parser cache. Deployment evidence is recorded in the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/1973-pinball/SSBM_STATS/releases/tag/v0.5.0).

## Added

- **Diamond Slippi and Master Slippi comparisons** join SSBMStats Community,
  Tournament, and Pro Tournament. Ranked references appear in headline, matchup,
  stage, move, execution, and interaction views. Benchmark highlighting and coach
  comparisons can use Diamond or Master; Tournament remains the default. Coach
  move comparisons use the matching character across all opponents and stages.
- Ranked references follow the exact selected character, opponent character, and
  stage where those controls apply. Community comparison controls include all
  26 playable characters even without local or community games. Matchups and
  moves present only in the ranked sample are visible too. An unavailable source
  or missing cell stays unavailable rather
  than substituting a broader sample.
- Every comparison keeps its measured sample and coverage. Unsupported attempt
  counts and unresolved outcomes show as unavailable; a measured zero remains
  zero. Community medians and their privacy thresholds are unchanged. Community
  interaction references remain unpublished.
- Ranked aggregates load by character and tier, independently of community and
  tournament services. Downloads are compressed and checked before use; the app
  does not download the replay corpus or bundle every ranked reference at startup.
- Execution charts start with your trend and one Tournament reference. A shared
  **Chart references** control can show any combination of the five cohorts, or
  hide their lines across the execution, move, and action charts. Table
  highlighting and opponent overlays keep their own controls. Selected sample
  scope stays visible, long explanations are expandable, and missing references
  remain explicit.

## Tournament and Pro updates included in this release

- **Tournament combines the former Venue and Tournament populations.** Shared
  games count once. The main archive retains its existing broad eligible
  population; the independently sampled interaction references now use their
  deduplicated union of **10,098 games / 20,196 player-games**.
- **Pro Tournament now includes 98 mapped players, 1,883 singles player-games,
  and 1,338 distinct games.** Reviewed identities expand the main archive from
  702 Pro singles player-games without adding replay files. Existing Pro
  interaction sample remains at those same 1,883 appearances, with coverage
  varying by metric.
- Aggregate Pro comparisons pool eligible sides before individual cells are
  suppressed for small samples, preserving valid pooled samples. Named-player
  comparisons remain available. Filters can show smaller samples, and a player-game means one
  player side, not one distinct game. Tournament and Pro populations can overlap.

See the [archive rebuild and publication report](../benchmark-baselines-release.md)
and [interaction sampling report](../interaction-benchmark-sample.md).

## Replay analysis and build tools

- Stats version 10 corrects headline statistics for recordings with missing end
  events or rewritten finalized history. It does not invent winners or treat
  incomplete evidence as valid special-move or interaction coverage. Ranked
  publication still excludes incomplete recordings and games without a normal,
  resolved end event. Win rates use only games with a determined result.
- Compatible saved statistics stay visible while newer results are prepared.
  **Existing users receive automatic analysis updates** when their replay files
  are accessible. The queue covers the saved library, including older games
  outside the current filters, using small batches and at most two workers.
  Completed replacements are saved before they appear, and unfinished work
  resumes on return. A browser that has lost folder permission still requires
  a reconnect; there is no separate one-time execution-refresh step.
  Interaction detector version 9 is unchanged, and no cache reset is needed.
- Dataset maintainers can pause and resume downloads and parsing, including
  automatic pauses after a chosen number of source archives. Verified downloads,
  partial downloads, and parser checkpoints are retained. The controller saves
  the source selection and settings, prevents competing controllers for one
  cache, and drains active work on pause or failure. A completed local build
  still requires separate verification and publication.

## Ranked sample and limitations

The release selects **130 complete archives** from
[erickfm/melee-ranked-replays at a pinned revision](https://huggingface.co/datasets/erickfm/melee-ranked-replays/tree/ef54ddc230e01373ff2a179b49a7e559af788081):
the smallest compressed archive for each of 26 character folders and five
rank-pair groups (Diamond–Diamond, Master–Master, Master–Diamond,
Diamond–Platinum, and Master–Platinum).
This is a deterministic convenience sample, not the complete source corpus, a
random sample, or a picture of today's ladder. Tier membership comes from each
actual player slot. Platinum opponents can appear in these games without adding
a Platinum baseline.

Eligible recordings are completed human singles with four starting stocks on
standard stages, lasting 30 seconds to 8 minutes 10 seconds. Early quit-outs,
incomplete recordings, conflicting tier evidence, and failed parser checks are
excluded. Folder names guide source selection; actual replay settings determine
character membership.

Full-source inspection also found negative air-dodge counts from slippi-js 9.1.2.
An export eligibility rule excludes both sides of affected games, keeping
complete measured samples without clamping counts or changing the parser.
The upstream detector remains unchanged; nonnegative counts are not proven
error-free. These exclusions are concentrated in some characters and add to the
sample's selection bias. The export excludes 320 games containing 321
negative side measurements. The [methodology report](../ranked-baselines-release.md#invalid-upstream-air-dodge-counts)
records the audit and evidence limits. Independent source reconciliation confirms
the exclusions and all exported counters.

| Verified dataset measure | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Selected complete source archives | 130 |
| Eligible distinct games across the selected source | 74,910 |
| Diamond Slippi player-games | 55,993 |
| Diamond Slippi distinct games | 49,404 |
| Master Slippi player-games | 58,642 |
| Master Slippi distinct games | 49,365 |
| Rejected games | 36,222 |

Diamond and Master distinct-game counts can overlap. Duplicate replay copies,
including equivalent recordings with different metadata wrappers, count once
after verification. One game can contribute two eligible player sides.
The completed scan found 23,772 repeated byte-identical copies and no validated
metadata-wrapper duplicates. Both tiers cover all 26 playable characters. The dataset
contains 6,344 exact aggregate rows in 54 gzip shards totaling 10,562,606 bytes.

The source does not establish rank timing, season, replay dates, or unique-player
counts. These are historical source labels and comparisons of measured
player-games, not current ranks, player-balanced averages, or skill percentiles.
Only derived numerical aggregates are published; source replays and local audit
records stay out of the release.

The [ranked source, eligibility, aggregation, and reproduction guide](../ranked-baselines-release.md)
documents the pinned selection, completed-human-singles requirements, exclusions,
duplicate handling, metric coverage, and publication checks.
